Our story

Made by a dad,
for dads.

Hi, I'm Cem. I built Papa while my wife Yekbun was pregnant with our first son, Levi.

We're an expat couple in the Netherlands. Our first pregnancy was an incredible ride — and a confusing one. The Dutch system is brilliant, but nobody tells you how it works. Every app we tried spoke to mothers. None spoke to first-time dads trying to figure out kraamzorg, geboorteverlof, and what the hell a combinatietest is.

So I built what I wished I'd had. Papa writes each country from the inside — the real rules, the real numbers, the real brands — and says the quiet parts out loud.

Made with love in Amsterdam. Cem, Yekbun & Levi
